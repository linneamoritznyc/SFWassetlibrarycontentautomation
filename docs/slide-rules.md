# Linnea's rules for SFW slides, carousels and PowerPoints

Collected from Linnea's notes and her Canva edits of the Costa Rica carousel (30 Oct 2026). The same rules are in docs/REEL-KNOWLEDGE.md (Reel Studio); this file is the one-page version. Read it before building any slide or deck.

## Size and format
- Instagram carousel: 1080 x 1350 px (4:5). Reels and stories: 1080 x 1920 px.
- People read on their phones: text is never tiny. If something doesn't fit, cut words, never shrink the type.

## Font sizes (on a 1080 px wide slide)

| Text | Font | Size |
|---|---|---|
| Headline on the hook slide and info card | Montserrat Bold (700) | about 84 to 86 px |
| Headline on other slides | Montserrat Bold (700) | 66 to 73 px, never under 64 px |
| Small text (sublines, dates, places, lists, CTA) | Source Sans 3 Regular (400), or Montserrat Regular | 52 to 54 px, never under 50 px (absolute floor 42 px) |

- Montserrat Bold, not ExtraBold. Small text Regular, not SemiBold. Upright type, never italic.
- The headline is the biggest thing on the slide by far, with tight line spacing so it reads as one block. Small text is clearly second but still easy to read.

## Text boxes (every deck must be fully editable)
- The big font (headline) is one text box.
- The small text under it (sublines, dates, places, list lines, CTA) is one text box together.
- Never split the small lines into separate boxes. Never put the headline and the small text in the same box.
- Make the headline box tall enough for its text, so it never runs into the small-text box.
- Everything is its own object: photo, colour boxes as shapes, text boxes, flowers, logo. Never a flattened image of a slide.
- Name each object (Photo, Headline, Details, Green box, Hibiscus, SFW logo) so it is easy to find in Canva.

## Words
- Fewer words, one idea per slide.
- Cut throwaway sublines ("The microscope, every day.") and filler words ("too").
- Merge short facts onto one line: "18–29 January 2027, Armonía, Heredia".
- Practical info early: slide 2 is the info card (workshop name, place, dates, info session, register, link in bio). Repeat it as the last slide.
- The caption and the slides say the same thing.
- All copy follows VOICE.md.

## Colour boxes and photos
- Flat colour boxes with hard edges, in brand colours: Food Web Green #156826, Soil Brown #4F3433, Education Blue #3780B8, Organic Cream #F4F1EA. Text in cream, white or #333130.
- One colour box carries everything for that slide: headline, small text and logo.
- Boxes hug their text with even margins and sit tight in a corner. No big empty areas inside boxes.
- Banners sit above the photo, not on top of it. The photo starts below the banner, so faces and the compost stay fully visible.
- A box covers the top part only (about 40% of the slide); faces are never cut by a box.
- Info cards: the banner is only as tall as its text (about a third of the slide), so the photo gets two thirds. The one-line headline runs almost the full width.
- Photo-over-box slides: the photo fills the top ~63%, the colour box the rest, with even ~72 px margins.
- Side-band slides: a green band down the left (~420 px), the photo on the right.
- Crop photos so the people and the subject fill them: faces in the top part, the subject centred.

## Logo
- The white SFW logo always sits inside a colour box or band, never floating over faces or randomly in the middle.
- Hook slide: large (about 150 px wide) in the box's bottom right, level with the small text.
- Info card: about 155 px, vertically centred on the right side of the banner, next to the small text.
- Side band: top-left corner of the band, above the headline.

## Flower cutouts
- Corner accents (top right or bottom right), never touching text.
- They can be big and bleed off the slide's edge or corner, like a sticker on a print.
- A flower can overlap a box's corner and break out past its edge, so box and photo feel layered.
- On side-band slides, the heliconia sits across the seam between the band and the photo at the bottom.

## Linnea's Canva file wins
When Linnea has edited a deck in Canva, her file is the source of truth. Read her exact positions and sizes from it and rebuild on them. Never move back a flower or a logo she already placed.

## Never
- Gold, gradients, photo fades, vignettes, glow, film grain, tape strips, tilted photos, italic type.
- Text on a plain background with no photo or box design, slide counters ("2/4"), "Day 1" or date header tags.
- Childish graphics, mascots, AI-generated photos.
- Prices on the slides.
