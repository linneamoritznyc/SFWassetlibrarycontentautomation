## 1. What the Foundation Courses are

There are **four courses**:

1. Foundation Course 1: Introduction to the Soil Food Web
2. Foundation Course 2: BioComplete™ Compost
3. Foundation Course 3: BioComplete™ Liquid Amendments
4. Foundation Course 4: Microscopy

The sales page also lists a fifth "course" called "Foundation Courses" with 1 lesson. That entry is the certificate step
("Earn your awesome certificate / Complete all of the courses and unlock your awesome certificate"), not a course.
The FC-only page's "5 Courses" count is a page bug already flagged to Stephanie, so always say four courses.
