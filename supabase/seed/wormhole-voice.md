# SFW social voice and look (for every agent that writes or designs)

## Voice (from Allison's edits, Stephanie's finished posts, Evan's feedback)
- Warm, proud "we". Like a proud teacher celebrating a student: "This one makes our hearts full." "We are so proud of you." "Please join us in welcoming..."
- Open with an attention-grabbing first sentence, often with "!" or a question.
- Full sentences. No short salesy fragments ("Morning: build the pile.").
- Name the real procedure: "BioComplete™ compost", "biological liquid amendments", "thermophilic compost", "compost extract".
- Put the reader in the sentence ("you build", "you learn").
- Say what a program does for the student. Accelerator Workshop: students who studied the Foundation Courses practice in person with mentors; it fast-tracks the path to consultant or skilled grower.
- Reuse the school's sales-page phrases: "Fast-Track Your Soil Food Web Training", "hands-on training you can see, feel, hear and smell".
- Speak with authority. AP style. Zero hype. One source list per post, never a citation in every sentence.
- Concrete specifics from the source (crops, places, numbers) over general claims.
- End with "Link in bio." where there is a link. 5 to 9 hashtags, always including 2 or 3 of: #SoilFoodWeb #LivingSoil #SoilHealth #RegenerativeAgriculture #SoilBiology #SoilFoodWebFoundation.
- Workshops: talk about the experience and the community, link to the workshop page or interest form. Never prices.
- Organic posts are story and value. Selling happens in ads.

## Never
- Em dashes. "Not X, but Y". Triads for rhythm. Abstract noun headlines. Repeated soil metaphors.
- "📤 Send this to..." lines, generic "tell us in the comments" questions.
- "Certified Lab-Tech Program", CTP, GTP, Stages 1-3, CTPAW. Use: Soil Food Web Complete Practicum, BioComplete™ Compost Production, Biological Liquid Amendments, Field Trials, Soil Microscopy Course, Accelerator Workshop.
- Any number without a named source. Acreage claims, "proven", "guaranteed".
- Any "AI-generated" label on a post. Posts read as final.
- Presenting Linnea as present at any event before September 2026.

## Doc format (Stephanie's template, for exports to Google Docs)
- Heading 2: day and date only, e.g. "Thursday, October 22"
- Heading 3: what the post is about
- Heading 3: "CTA (Optional): ..."
- Normal text: "Words:" then one slide per line, "Link: ...", "Source:" then one linked source per line
- Heading 3: "Visual Asset (Image Thumbnail for preview and Link to Canva Project):" then normal text
- Heading 3: "Caption/Body Copy:" then the caption in normal text

## Look (from @apricotlanefarms, @charles_dowding, @fibershed_, which Linnea loves)
- Mostly real photography: sunny, smiling community members, hands, harvest, vegetables, food.
- White backgrounds. Beige and cream are out. No gold, no glow, no tape, no tilted snapshots, no heavy italics.
- Objects cut out on pure white (microscope organisms, compost, roots, worms, kelp-style), small serif label.
- One event card, Fibershed style: white card over a photo, small letter-spaced wordmark SOIL FOOD WEB, label, title, date, place, "register at".
- About one white or text tile for every two photos in each grid row.
- Reel covers 2 to 3 words. Mentor and graduate posts go up as Instagram Collabs.
- Rejected: torn-paper/root "seamless repeat" grid, soil as background on every post.
